# Privacy Policy for FSIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
FSIyagi is a partition format and filesystem driver for Linux and Windows.

## Data Collection
FSIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Files and settings stay on your own disks and PC. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
FSIyagi does not need an internet connection and does not send any data to external servers.
